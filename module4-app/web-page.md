## Headline
TrueFit: Your city. Your fit. An easier job search.

## Subhead
Tell us where you want to live and what you want to do. Get a short list of real entry-level business jobs that fit you, not hundreds of random listings you're not interested in or qualified for.

## The problem, in the user's words
"I put in entry level, full time, my salary, in person, my city, my field, and I still get way too many jobs. Half of them say entry level and then ask for three years of experience. I can't search for analyst and finance and research at the same time, so I'm on four sites scrolling forever."

## How the app helps
- **Starts with your city:** search Cincinnati, Chicago, Charlotte, Detroit or Charleston.
- **Searches several job titles at once** and only shows jobs that match your experience level, so "entry level" jobs asking for years of experience stay out.
- **Matches what you want:** answer 5 quick questions (kind of work, company size, industry, culture, benefits) and see why each job fits.

Why it matters: about 1 in 4 "entry-level" jobs ask for at least a year of experience (Hirebase, 2026), and job seekers name irrelevant results and poor filters as top problems on major job sites (MeasuringU, 2019).

**Try it:** pick your city and see your matches.
