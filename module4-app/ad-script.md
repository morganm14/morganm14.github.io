# Ad script

> DRAFT, written before the app is built. Check against the finished app before using.

## Length
30 seconds

## The purpose (what the ad has to get across)
- Job searching should be easier
- Search in the city you want to live in
- See only jobs you're interested in and qualified for, not random ones

## The scenes
| Seconds | On screen | Voice or text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | A college senior scrolling job listings fast. Random jobs fly by: wrong city, wrong field, "3+ years experience required." | Voice: "Wrong city. Wrong field. Not qualified." | Upbeat music, a buzzer sound on each bad job |
| 5–10 | The senior puts the phone down, frustrated. Text pops up over hundreds of results. | Text: "Job searching shouldn't feel like this." | Music cuts out |
| 10–15 | The app opens. They tap their city: Cincinnati, Chicago, Charlotte, Detroit or Charleston. | Voice: "Start with where you want to live." | Music comes back, a soft tap sound |
| 15–22 | They tap job titles they actually want, set max experience to 0–1 years, then answer quick questions on the kind of work, industry and culture they want. | Voice: "Tell it what you want to do and what you're ready for." | Tap sounds in rhythm |
| 22–27 | Tap **Find my jobs**. A short list appears, each job with a "why it fits" line. The senior smiles. | Voice: "Only jobs that fit you. No random listings. No jobs you're not qualified for." | A "ding" when results appear |
| 27–30 | TrueFit logo and tagline. | Voice + text: "TrueFit. Your city. Your fit. An easier job search." | Music ends on a beat |

## Tone
Fast

## Checked against solution.md
- City choice, job titles, max experience, "what you want" questions, "why it fits": all in solution.md
- "No jobs you're not qualified for" relies on the max-experience filter, and some postings don't state experience. Soften to "fewer" if testing shows unqualified jobs slipping through.
- Left out on purpose: "saves you 5–7 hours" (no source yet) and Indeed (not in the collected listings)
- App name: TrueFit
