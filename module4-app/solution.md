# The solution

> All decisions made. Survey answers (due Thursday) may still change this plan.

## In one sentence
A phone-friendly job search that lets a business senior describe what they want (several job titles, must-haves, and what they like doing) and returns 20–25 entry-level jobs that really fit, with fake "entry-level" jobs filtered out.

## What someone does with it
1. Opens the app on their phone
2. Picks one or more job titles (e.g., financial analyst, marketing coordinator, event coordinator)
3. Sets must-haves: city, in person, full time, minimum salary, max experience required (0–1 year)
4. Answers 5 "what you want" questions: kind of work, company size, industry, culture, benefits
5. Taps **Find my jobs**
6. Sees a list of up to 25 matches, best fit first, each with a short "why it fits" line
7. Taps a job to see details, then taps **Apply on original site**

## The screens
1. **Job titles**
   - Shows: a list of business job titles, grouped:
     - Finance & analysis: financial analyst, business/data analyst, research analyst, credit analyst
     - Marketing: marketing coordinator/associate, digital marketing
     - Operations: operations analyst, supply chain analyst
     - Sales: sales/business development, account representative
     - Events: event coordinator/associate, events marketing, brand activation/experiential marketing, corporate travel/incentive trip coordinator, fundraising/special events coordinator
   - Tap: select as many as you want → **Next**
2. **Must-haves**
   - Shows: city, work type (in person), full time, minimum salary, max years of experience
   - Tap: set each one → **Next**
3. **What you want**
   - Shows: 5 quick questions, tap to pick one or more answers (answer choices are a first guess — edit them):
     - **Kind of work:** numbers/analysis, people/clients, creative/marketing, research, operations, events/experiences
     - **Company size:** small (under 100), mid (100–1,000), large (1,000+), no preference
     - **Industry:** banking/finance, healthcare, consumer goods, tech, consulting, nonprofit, sports, no preference
     - **Culture:** collaborative, independent, fast-paced, structured, no preference
     - **Benefits:** health insurance, 401(k) match, tuition help, paid time off, training program
   - Tap: answer → **Find my jobs**
4. **Results**
   - Shows: up to 25 jobs, each with title, company, salary, experience required, and "why it fits"
   - Tap a job → Job detail
   - Tap **Edit search** → back to screen 1
5. **Job detail**
   - Shows: full description, salary, experience required, which criteria it matches
   - Tap **Apply on original site** → opens the listing on LinkedIn or TeamWork Online

## What it does not do
- Does not apply for you
- No resume or cover letter help
- No accounts or logins
- No saved jobs list
- No interview prep or networking

## Where the information comes from
- **What the person types in:** job titles, must-haves, answers to the "what you want" questions
- **What the app already knows:** a hand-collected set of 15–20 real entry-level business listings per city (75–100 total), plus 5–8 event-focused listings per city from LinkedIn and TeamWork Online (stored in listings.csv), for Cincinnati, Chicago, Charlotte, Detroit and Charleston
  - Each listing is tagged with: title, company, city, salary, years of experience required, kind of work, company size, industry, culture, benefits, and a link to the original posting
