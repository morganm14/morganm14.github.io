Questions

Good to know

Who is TrueFit for?

Senior business majors looking for a full-time, in-person, entry-level job in Cincinnati, Chicago, Charlotte, Detroit or Charleston. It's built for people who are open to more than one job title.

Where do the jobs come from?

Real listings collected by hand from LinkedIn and TeamWork Online: 91 so far, about 18 per city, including event-focused jobs. Job sites don't let outside apps pull listings automatically, so each one was gathered and tagged by hand.

How does it filter out fake "entry-level" jobs?

You set the most experience you're ready for (0–1 year), and TrueFit leaves out jobs that ask for more. Some postings don't list experience at all, so they're marked "not stated" and you can decide.

What job titles can I search?

Finance & analysis (financial, business/data, research and credit analyst), marketing (coordinator/associate, digital), operations (operations and supply chain analyst), sales (business development, account representative) and events (event coordinator, events marketing, brand activation, corporate travel and fundraising/special events).

Do I need an account?

No. There are no logins or accounts. Open it, answer the questions and see your matches.

How do I apply?

Tap a job, then tap Apply on original site. That opens the posting on LinkedIn or TeamWork Online, and you apply there.
