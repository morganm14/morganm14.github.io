## The three calls worth reviewing

1. **Every course title is at 80% of demand's projection. This goes against `demand`.** Rule 5 runs out before the budget does. Even with every title at its 80% floor and nothing held back for escalations, the order would be $27,511.91. The extra units would mostly be unrecoverable after 2027-01-09 (Microeconomics, Calculus) or not returnable at all (codes, calculator, packet), and that drove the cut. Expect stockouts in week one:
   - Statistics: 16 codes short. This is the cheapest gap to close, because codes have a 0-day lead time.
   - Microeconomics: 13 copies short. No reorder can be filled after 2027-02-01.
   - Biology: 10 short.
   - The other titles: 3 to 6 short each.
2. **On Accounting, Calculus, Marketing and Ethics I cut the used line, which `margin` shows as the best earner, instead of the lowest-margin line.** Used earns $76.00, $109.25, $70.06 and $33.00 a copy on those four titles. Rule 5 says to cut the lowest-margin line first. On these four titles, though, the new and rental lines are pinned at the publisher minimums in `risk.md` (20, 20, 12 and 25 copies). Cutting them would have meant ordering below the minimum, which is not an order the publisher accepts. So the cut fell on used. This also lowers our reliance on buyback copies, which `margin` says it cannot count.
3. **Ethics is 25 new and 0 used, where `demand`'s fall mix calls for 13 new and 16 used.** The publisher minimum is 25 new (`risk.md`). I held the title at its 29-copy projection rather than adding used copies on top, so 29 − 25 = 4 used. Rule 5 then cut those 4 because the min-25 line could not move. The 12 new copies above the mix stay returnable until 2027-02-06 ($604.80). The publisher also notes Ethics is "widely available secondhand off campus." If students go elsewhere, those copies go back.

Also applied, though it does not go against an analyst: Microeconomics has no rental copies. Its 10 rental units went to new because a single spring rental earns −$66.48 before the 10th edition ships (`margin.md`).
